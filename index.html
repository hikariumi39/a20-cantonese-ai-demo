Fixed scoring rubric: five independent 1–7 ratings

Expressive suppressionInhibiting outward emotional expression
Experiential avoidanceEscaping or controlling unwanted inner experience
AcceptanceAllowing inner experience without trying to remove it
Cognitive reappraisalChanging the interpretation of the event
Distress toleranceEnduring distress while continuing purposeful action

How to read a score

1Very weak
2–3Weak
4Moderate
5–6Strong
7Very strong

Each score indicates how strongly the scenario text supports that construct under the fixed rubric. Scores are not percentages, probabilities, diagnoses or personality traits. The five ratings are independent: compare JAN and TAIHOI within each construct; do not add them into a total score.
